## Problem
In the web version, looking at a lower floor, "you can still see the hovering caution icons generated by rooms on higher floors."

## Context
- The ⚠ badges are made in `src/render3d/stage3d.ts` (around line 298, `statusBadge("⚠")`, added to `badges` at each room label's position).
- `applyFloorCut` hides what's above the picked floor by `o.userData.floor` while traversing `holeGroup`. The badges likely carry no floor (or sit outside `holeGroup`), so they're never hidden. The labels themselves may already handle this; follow how they do it.
- Check Godot for the same (it may draw badges from the bridge's info).

## Approach
Tag each badge with its room's floor and hide it with the floor cut (and on rebuild). Done: picking a floor shows only that floor's and deeper floors' badges.
