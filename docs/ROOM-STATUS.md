# Room status: built vs described

Every room the catalog (ROOMS.md) describes, and whether it's in the game (`data/rooms.json`). Generated by `node scripts/room-status.mjs`; rerun it after adding rooms. What an unbuilt room needs first, and why a room is parked, are kept by hand in the script.

**At a glance:** 65 room types built. Of the 95 rooms in the catalog's tables, 58 are built, 4 are parked and 33 are still to build. Of those, 0 need nothing new (A), 5 need one new resource, effect or unlock (B), and 27 need a new system (C).

**Needs, graded:** **A** uses what the game already has (resources, effects, rules). **B** needs one new resource, effect type or unlock. **C** needs a whole new system (goods, tourism, safety, commutes, H-size rooms…). **Parked** rooms stay in the catalog but aren't planned, each with why (see below); unpark one by taking it out of `PARKED` in the script and its "(parked)" mark out of ROOMS.md.
